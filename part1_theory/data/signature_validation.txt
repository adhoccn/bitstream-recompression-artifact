[DIAGNOSIS]
  The fundamental does NOT decay as 1/Delta: it equals N to within a
  few percent for every lattice tested.  Under the exact comb model
  hat h(M) = hat w(0) = N, i.e. the comb replicates the DC term onto
  the fundamental index, so the Theta(1/Delta) claim of Prop 4 and the
  Theta(eps) claim of Prop 7 are NOT consequences of Theorem 1 alone.
  The two statements require an additional assumption, stated in the
  revised proofs: the comb must be read against a *within-bin*
  reference, i.e. the idle-tone model below.
