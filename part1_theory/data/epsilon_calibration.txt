Reading:
  * eps_nominal is what a CRF-based reading of Theorem 6 would assume.
  * eps_rms is what Theorem 6 actually sees after pooling frames, since
    H^2 accumulates additively and H^2 ~ C eps^2 per frame.
  * N*/N*_nom is the resulting distortion of the barrier: values far
    from 1 mean the CRF axis cannot be substituted for the step axis.
  * P(eps<=0) is the fraction of frame pairs for which the SECOND step
    is not larger than the first, i.e. the q2>q1 regime of Theorem 1
    does not hold frame-locally even when dCRF > 0.

Worked example at dCRF = 3 (the empirical study's 'Delta CRF <= 3'):
  Qstep ratio from CRF alone      : 2^(3/6) = 1.4142 -> eps = 0.4142
    dQP_eff = -3  ->  q2/q1 = 0.7071  ->  eps = -0.2929
    dQP_eff = +0  ->  q2/q1 = 1.0000  ->  eps = +0.0000
    dQP_eff = +3  ->  q2/q1 = 1.4142  ->  eps = +0.4142
    dQP_eff = +6  ->  q2/q1 = 2.0000  ->  eps = +1.0000
    dQP_eff = +9  ->  q2/q1 = 2.8284  ->  eps = +1.8284
  A frame-type/AQ swing of only +-3 QP therefore moves eps by more than
  a factor of two at dCRF = 3, and a swing of -6 QP flips its sign.
